GuideSplit

Guide-based image splitter · browser-only

1 · Upload image

The file stays in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

2 · Place guides

Upload an image to begin.

Drag from the top ruler to add a vertical guide. Drag from the left ruler to add a horizontal guide. Drag a guide to reposition it. Image edges are always split boundaries.

3 · Split preview

No output areas yet.

Vertical guides split columns; horizontal guides split rows. Three vertical guides create four output images.

4 · Export

Upload an image to startPlace divider guides at the exact pixels where the image should be cut. The image edges plus your guides become the exported regions.
